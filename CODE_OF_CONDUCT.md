# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for everyone interested in learning and contributing to Data Structures & Algorithms.

We want this repository to remain a positive space for learning, collaboration, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributors and community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Help others learn and grow.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Provide constructive and relevant feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on learning and improvement.
- Give appropriate credit when using or building upon someone else's work.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Offensive, abusive, or inappropriate language.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or contributions.
- Publishing private or personal information without permission.
- Plagiarism or claiming someone else's work as your own.
- Any behavior that negatively impacts the learning community.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions on participation in the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainer through the contact information available in the repository.

All reports will be handled respectfully and, where appropriate, confidentially.

---

## Final Note

The goal of this repository is to create a positive environment where developers can:

- 📚 Learn Data Structures & Algorithms
- 🧠 Improve problem-solving skills
- ☕ Practice Java
- 🤝 Share knowledge
- 🚀 Grow together

Thank you for helping make this project welcoming, respectful, and professional.

**Happy Coding! 🚀**
